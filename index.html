Ti-60 Diffusion Bonding & Abaqus FEA

Conducted at Northwestern Polytechnical University (NPU). Investigating solid-state diffusion bonding mechanisms of near-alpha Ti-60 titanium alloys for high-temperature aerospace structures.

NPU MATERIALS SCIENCE RESEARCH GROUP 2024 – PRESENT

Multiscale Optimization of Joint Reliability via Coupled Experimental Testing & Numerical Simulation

Diffusion bonding offers superior strength-to-weight ratios for aerospace skin-stringer and turbine assemblies by eliminating the melting phase. This research combines physical mechanical tests with Abaqus Finite Element Analysis to analyze how process variables govern void coalescence, atomic interdiffusion, and grain growth across the contact interface.

1. Experimental Metallurgy & Joint Testing
Subjecting Ti-60 specimens to controlled thermal-mechanical profiles in high-vacuum environments to measure macroscopic shear and tensile joint performance.
2. Non-Linear Abaqus FEA Modeling
Simulating local surface roughness asperities, plastic creep deformation, and interfacial contact pressure distribution across micro-scale contact zones.
3. Automated Python Pipeline
Engineered Python scripts within Abaqus CAE to automate parametric sweeps, extract nodal stress/strain histories, and compute interfacial void closure rates.
